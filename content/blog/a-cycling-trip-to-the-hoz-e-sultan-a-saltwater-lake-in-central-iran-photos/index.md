*Cycling in Iran deserts, Hoz Sultan saltwater lake*

Hoz e Sultan (حوض سلطان / [google maps](https://goo.gl/maps/Uk6S7xRrJHYvrCoQA)) which means “Sultans Pool” in Farsi is located 85 KM south of Tehran. It’s a saltwater lake in the middle of a desert so its dry during the summer and salt harvesters are busy exploiting it.

But in this sunny late November weekend and after two weeks of rain we decided that its the best time to visit the place and it was indeed.

{{< youtube TxjeICGdSx4 >}}

To reach the starting point, you have to drive around 2 hours from Tehran. Catch the Persian Gulf highway and you will reach Haft-Aseman Complex (its called a complex, but do not expect a huge building!). Refill the gas, park your cars and make your bikes ready.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-02.jpeg)

We did not faces any cars (except our support cars) during the first half of the road. The road is kind of bumpy but not unpleasant. Oh… we saw a group of camels too.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-03.jpeg)

After a left turn, we are on a smaller road with Pistachio trees on both sides. But scenery kept alternating between short trees and the desert.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-04.jpeg)

A couple of more KMs and the salts start appearing.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-05.jpeg)

and soon…

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-06.jpeg)

and after another 30 mins of cycling, we reached the lake. On the map this area is shown in blue but at least in this season the road is 100% dry but if you leave the road and enter the “lake”, the depth will be no more than 30cm. We’ve been warned that the salt can penetrate into every single part of our bike and ruin it but… it's impossible to lose the opportunity. we said “I’ll wash it later” and entered the lake.

After some photos we continued for another 10 minutes and reached our final destination.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-07.jpeg)

{{< youtube RLkmd-RB0WE >}}

Which is a salt harvesting facility. For me this looks like a view from a ski place or a south pole station.

There were a few offroad cars in the area and some groups were camping. We climbed the salt hills (with and without bikes), had our lunch and relaxed for an hour.

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-10.jpeg)

![A Cycling Trip To The Hoz e Sultan; A saltwater lake in central Iran (Photos)](img-11.jpeg)

At around 14:00, we returned back. It took around 1:30 each way without much elevation gain / loose. But please note that we were a 40+ people group.

{{< youtube SZaGvdIri4U >}}

## Notes

- You can not buy anything on the way. Pack anything you need.
- The road is bumpy and offroad like. Not suitable for road bikes; although not impossible.
- Desert can be windy & cold. Have jackets and wind breakers.
- There is no toilet facilities.
- Time to time huge truck might pass. They are transporting trucks.
- No entrance or parking fee.
- This is the track on strava: [https://strava.app.link/3Ew3ZcVfAlb](https://strava.app.link/3Ew3ZcVfAlb); 40 KM and around 3 Hours.
- I would say the difficulty was moderate. Not the best place for first timers but whoever does cycling time to time, will be able to finish this too.
